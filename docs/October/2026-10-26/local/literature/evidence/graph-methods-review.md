# Graph, measurement and neuroscience evidence note

Coordinator audit, 2026-10-11. Companion [records](../../../../../../doc/literature/evidence/graph-methods-references.json) contain 28 works. Five have substantive selected-section readings: T-GRAB, Arroyo, Chehreghani, Gao and Recanatesi. Floquet's historical paper and Roy–Vetterli are metadata-level foundations; the remaining records are contextual abstract-level checks. Download availability is handled centrally and does not upgrade these labels.

The consequential graph overlaps and their limitations are integrated in [the novelty report](../novelty-review.md), Section 2. The source snapshots remain unchanged. The public T-GRAB record is a predecessor with the owner's coauthorship, not independent evidence produced in this session.

Passage map:

- T-GRAB v2: Sections 3.1–3.3, the periodic counting/memorizing and later diagnostic task definitions; compare graph forecasting objectives with the proposed cue-retention objective.
- Arroyo v2: Section 3 vectorized Jacobian/recurrent connection; Section 4.1 Propositions 4.3–4.4 and Section 4.2 measurements. The uniform contraction/common-zero assumptions must accompany any invocation.
- Chehreghani v1: Sections 3.2–3.3 definitions and Section 4 noisy update; its geometric guarantee expressly excludes a performance guarantee. Fresh noise and retained history are different sources of variance.
- Gao: introduction/theory overview and Supplement II.II participation-ratio definition; task sampling and temporal smoothness are interpretation controls.
- Recanatesi: Introduction; Results “The theoretical framework,” Equations 1–12, and motif-expansion discussion. The linearized point-process setting is not a theorem about a trained gated recurrent model's useful memory.

The graph architecture entries cover GC-LSTM, T-GCN, EvolveGCN, DCRNN and TGN; native vision context includes ConvLSTM, ConvGRU and PredRNN. Their inclusion documents architecture/state provenance. It does not claim that all these papers studied collapse or that their tasks can be substituted for a memory experiment without changing the question.

Neural collapse during terminal supervised training and contrastive dimensional collapse are included to disambiguate terminology. Randomized linear algebra and effective-rank references establish measurement antecedents. Their algorithms need separate implementation validation and do not supply causal memory claims.

Metadata were taken from arXiv citation records, official proceedings and publishers; Crossref resolved selected non-arXiv records.
