## Does success answer the question and retain a negative?

The experiment can identify the **total effect of the specified assigned body
ordering** at fixed weights and equal horizon. It cannot separately identify a
pure periodicity mechanism: the current controls necessarily differ in registered
final-motif exposure and can differ in other higher-order statistics. This is an
accepted interpretation limit, not grounds to invalidate or silently redesign
the frozen contrast ([scope:20](ordering-mechanism-scope-independent-review-2026-10-16.md#L20),
[scope:43](ordering-mechanism-scope-independent-review-2026-10-16.md#L43)). Input
FFT/covariance cannot substitute for cue-conditioned state or recovery.

The publication rules admit preservation and precise bounded equivalence, rather
than requiring collapse ([charter:281](../../../2026-10-26/local/auto-research/research-charter.md#L281)). Short-horizon
failure leaves acquisition, native access or optimization unresolved; equal long-horizon forgetting
can be shared forgetting; a CI containing zero is inconclusive. Long-horizon
failure must remain reportable and must not be a checkpoint-selection objective.
A near-zero contrast at chance or a loss ceiling cannot establish intact storage.
Equivalence needs the frozen margin, valid tests and multiplicity treatment;
ten seeds are a proposal, not assured precision
([design:537](../../../2026-10-26/local/auto-research/experimental-design-v2.md#L537)). Conclusions are conditional on
the declared competence/selection policy; failed confirmation seeds cannot be
silently discarded.

Novelty still needs a held-out prediction or causal boundary beyond the close
periodic-attractor and task-sensitive-memory antecedents. More architectures,
Floquet spectra or rank curves alone are insufficient
([audit:27](../../../2026-10-26/local/literature/novelty-review.md#L27),
[audit:88](../../../2026-10-26/local/literature/novelty-review.md#L88)). This review does not refresh the
October 11 literature search or grant a priority certificate.

## Three changes to freeze before a training request

1. **Publish a versioned release/task map.** Separate prospective original graph
   reconstruction, its finite native parity check, the unchanged vector Gate B,
   and a later graph required-history intervention. Define what each node can
   establish; require trusted loading, full-state chronology and no test exposure
   before releasing a graph fit. A graph memory node must specify the varied
   history, native query/target dependency and short-retention acquisition/readout
   tests; no arbitrary F1 threshold should be invented here.
2. **Register the claim as the assigned-order contrast.** Carry the accepted
   motif-exposure limitation into the run's estimand/interpretation packet;
   retain realized input diagnostics and simpler readout/predictability
   alternatives. Do not adjust away treatment-derived descriptors post hoc or
   replace the treatment with a multi-motif superperiod without a separately
   reviewed version. Preserve both loss and attractor-memory outcomes.
3. **Freeze a staged decision/admission packet.** Before discovery, pin the task,
   source/config, selection and failure rules, native call counts, outputs and
   finite resource/stop envelope. Explicitly label competence diagnosis as
   discovery. Before confirmation, separately freeze the SEI units (continuous
   normalized MSE versus raw MSE), family, valid primary test assumptions,
   equivalence rule, failed-seed handling and power/precision criterion. This
   prevents requiring a confirmation analysis before a tiny diagnostic, while
   preventing discovery scores from becoming confirmatory evidence
   ([design:571](../../../2026-10-26/local/auto-research/experimental-design-v2.md#L571),
   [design:592](../../../2026-10-26/local/auto-research/experimental-design-v2.md#L592)).
