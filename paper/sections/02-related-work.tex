\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Periodic and Floquet memory; multistability.}
Linear stability of periodic systems is classical \citep{floquet1883}, and the discrete period map is the standard tool for cycle stability. In recurrent networks, \citet{pals2024-phase-locked-memory} show that transient cue identities select stable phase-locked limit cycles in a periodically driven trained RNN, analysed with Poincar\'e maps and Floquet multipliers; this is both a direct antecedent and a counterexample to reading local contraction as forgetting. \citet{ashwin2023-echo-repetitions} relate input repetitions and symbol dwell to transitions in the echo index, the number of attracting input-driven responses \citep{ceni2020}. Periodic drive can also suppress chaos and phase-lock random circuits \citep{rajan2010}, and orthogonal rotations implement delayed copying as an internal clock \citep{henaff2016-orthogonal-memory}. Delta we must demonstrate: that an equal-horizon ordering intervention in a verified-competent model changes recoverability of written cues, beyond echo-index multiplicity and phase-locked memory, and that a theory predicts both preservation and loss.

\paragraph{Echo states and fading memory.}
Echo states are functions of the left-infinite input history, and forgetting the initial condition is distinct from retaining input history \citep{jaeger2001,jaeger2002}. \citet{manjunath2013} give input-specific echo-state conditions; under a uniform shrinking bound the driven response is unique and, for a periodic driver, has a period dividing the input period. Capacity of linear readouts over state is quantified by \citet{dambre2012-information-capacity} and \citet{white2004}, and driven random networks can be locally expansive yet non-chaotic with improved sequence memory \citep{schuecker2018}. Delta: separate washout of initial conditions from loss of a written cue using cue-conditioned clouds at fixed body history; a unique forced response is the synchronization hypothesis H3, not evidence of collapse.

\paragraph{Memory geometry and Fisher memory.}
\citet{ganguli2008-memory-traces} measure retained input information relative to specified dynamical noise with the Fisher memory matrix and distinguish normal from nonnormal recurrence; \citet{ganguli2010-compressed-memory} show structured signals can be reconstructed beyond naive dimension counts. Noise structure changes linear memory \citep{guan2025-noise-memory}, continuous attractors have fundamental noise limits \citep{burak2012,koyluoglu2017}, and analog memory as a line attractor requires neutral directions \citep{seung1996}. \citet{sharma2026-temporal-superposition} analyse delayed recall through age-indexed features, interference and useful forgetting. Covariance spectra of recurrent networks \citep{hu2022} and the entropy effective rank \citep{roy2007effective} are the measurement antecedents for the participation ratio and entropy dimension used here. Delta: a task-sensitive channel model with a declared noise location must predict held-out failures better than horizon, norm, participation ratio and readout baselines, and must be tested by direction-specific interventions.

\paragraph{Temporal correlations and learning.}
\citet{fokam2026-temporal-correlations} show that temporally correlated inputs change learned memory tradeoffs in linear recurrent networks, with a local release boundary at lag one and explicit qualifications under strong periodicity; \citet{proca2025-linear-learning} and \citet{bordelon2025} analyse learning dynamics of temporal features and slow modes. Optimization obstacles for long dependencies are classical \citep{bengio1994-long-term-dependencies,pascanu2013-training-rnns} and motivated gated \citep{hochreiter1997-lstm} and unitary \citep{arjovsky2016-unitary-rnn} architectures. Delta: our intervention freezes weights and changes the body ordering at inference; training-correlation effects remain a named alternative unless checkpoints and training distributions are controlled, and failed acquisition is a competing explanation that the competence gate must exclude.

\paragraph{Graph recurrence and oversmoothing.}
Graph-state recurrence dates to \citet{scarselli2009gnn}; spectral conditions for exponential information loss in deep GCNs \citep{oono2020exponential}, time-varying attention propagation \citep{wu2023attention}, rank-collapse accounts of oversmoothing and over-correlation \citep{roth2024rank,roth2024splitting}, oscillatory propagation \citep{rusch2022graphcon}, and the explicit bridge between GNN Jacobians and recurrent models \citep{arroyo2025bridging} are established. \citet{chehreghani2026noise} show persistent Gaussian perturbations sustain Dirichlet energy without guaranteeing downstream utility. The temporal graph benchmark T-GRAB \citep{dizaji2025tgrab} defines periodic counting and memorizing tasks $P_{\mathrm{det}}(k,n)$, delayed cause--effect and long-range tasks, and evaluates GC-LSTM \citep{chen2022gclstm} alongside event-based and evolving models \citep{rossi2020tgn,pareja2020evolvegcn,zhao2020tgcn,li2018dcrnn,gravina2024ctan,yi2025tgbseq}. Delta: reproduce the predecessor task with a complete-state adapter (state dimension $2NH$ for a one-layer node-wise LSTM on $N$ nodes), test independently cued memory rather than next-graph prediction, and show a forced temporal mechanism that \citet{arroyo2025bridging} do not already cover.

\paragraph{Recurrent depth and looped transformers.}
Weight-tied depth recurrence \citep{universal_transformer,albert}, equilibrium models \citep{deep_equilibrium}, programmable loops \citep{looped_programmable}, timestep encoding \citep{looped_expressive_timestep}, recurrent-depth reasoning with reported state collapse \citep{huginn}, memory highways \citep{mesh}, gated latent loops \citep{loopus} and fixed-point reasoners \citep{fixed_point_reasoners} form a crowded field. \citet{looped_stability_recall} separates autonomous iteration from input recall and derives the equilibrium input sensitivity $(I-J)^{-1}B$; \citet{think_shallow_solve_deep} relate QR Lyapunov estimates, dimension proxies and nonnormality to test-time depth. Delta: a depth loop that re-reads a whole motif has forcing period one loop and persistent input access, so it cannot inherit the streaming mechanism; we treat it as a separate branch and state the reinjection identity (P10) as an antecedent, not a result.

\paragraph{Collapse diagnostics across architectures.}
Neural collapse \citep{papyan2020neuralcollapse} and dimensional collapse \citep{jing2022dimensional} name training-time phenomena. Rank collapse in attention \citep{pure_attention_rank,transformer_signal_rank}, its qualification by value and projection spectra \citep{transformer_oversmoothing_record}, exact-rank and identifiability analyses \citep{collapse_symmetry_rank}, token dynamics of selective state-space models \citep{mamba_token_dynamics}, and the architecture-general routing-collapse claim \citep{all_routes_collapse} address iterate or token clouds. Modern recurrent families \citep{s4,mamba,mamba2_ssd,xlstm,rwkv7,gated_delta,block_recurrent_transformer} differ in which quantities are persistent state, and copying bounds \citep{repeat_after_me} and finite-precision results \citep{weiss2018-finite-precision,korsky2019-rnn-power} supply competing capacity explanations for long-horizon failure. Delta: an operational definition of collapse in terms of required information, a complete-state budget that counts every memory path, and a demonstrated failure in competent models before any architecture ranking.

\paragraph{Dimensionality in neuroscience.}
Task complexity and sampling constrain measured neural dimension \citep{gao2017dimensionality}; recurrent connectivity shapes covariance dimensionality in spiking networks \citep{recanatesi2019dimensionality} and across cortical areas \citep{dahmen2026dimensionality}; low-rank and correlated structure produce low-dimensional dynamics \citep{mastrogiuseppe2018,schuessler2020}; working-memory representations vary with task factors \citep{orhan2019-memory-representations}; and input fluctuations reduce attractor dimension of chaotic networks \citep{engelken2023}. Delta: recurrence-controls-dimension is prior art; we must add the task-required information and its causal use.

\paragraph{Probing, interventions and numerics.}
Control tasks \citep{hewitt2019-control-probes} and task-relevance controls \citep{ravichander2021-probe-relevance} separate decodability from use. Input-driven Lyapunov methodology for RNNs \citep{vogt2020} and randomized linear algebra \citep{halko2011randomized,meyer2021hutchpp} supply the finite-time and matrix-free tools. Delta: full-state tangent products validated against dense references on small cases, finite-difference and JVP agreement at declared tolerances, and causal interventions with equal-energy shams rather than probe accuracy alone.
