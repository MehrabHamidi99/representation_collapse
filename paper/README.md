# Recurrent-memory diagnostic manuscript

**When Does Repetition Erase Memory? Identifiability Limits for Recurrent-State Collapse**

A venue-neutral ML manuscript with a compact main text, references and detailed
appendices. The checked build has eight main-text pages and 23 total pages,
including the four-page graph appendix (pages 19–22). It replaces the earlier
35-page research-program skeleton. The scientific scope is a diagnostic framework: analytic ordering
nulls and counterexamples, a controlled experimental design, and a verified
four-cell pilot audit, thirty-two local acquisition fits, and a separately
verified single-seed corrected temporal-graph reconstruction at two training
budgets in an appendix. Eight singleton
models pass their individual gates; the original joint competence requirement
remains unmet. It does not claim successful learned-memory erasure,
an empirical equivalence result, or a new general Floquet theorem.

The October 20 long fit completed 200 epochs / 511,800 updates, with validation selecting
epoch 160. Frozen within-motif F1 declines 0.959297 → 0.818771 (14.05 pp).
Complete-state PR declines modestly, variance attenuates within motifs, and
pooling changes the interpretation. The earlier seven-epoch pilot remains unchanged;
these are one seed/graph, below Persistence overall, not independent replications
or the full Figure 10. See the
[verified long-fit report](../doc/auto/figure10-gclstm-longfit-results-2026-10-20.md).

## Structure

The main text proceeds through motivation, measurement targets, analytic
constraints, matched-order design, analytic controls and the competence audit,
related work, limitations, and discussion/conclusion. Appendices contain full
proofs, exact task/training settings, all 32 cue-conditioned cloud measurements,
the probe-floor derivation, zero-body acquisition controls, the corrected
temporal-graph reconstruction and provenance. The earlier internal critique of
inherited manuscripts is retained in `doc/auto/source-inventory.md` and
`doc/auto/theory-propositions.md`; it is not a paper appendix.

## Figures and traceability

- `figures/design.tex`: vector schematic of WRITE/body/QUERY pairing.
- `figures/analytic_controls.pdf`: deterministic analytic constructions
  separating dimension, local contraction, readout offsets and noise-dependent
  ordering risk. These are not measurements of trained networks.
- `figures/pilot_audit.pdf`: native behavior from the verified
  `doc/auto/initial-phase-results.json`; one training seed per cell, no
  fabricated training-seed intervals.
- `figures/figure10-reconstruction.pdf`: the new one-hour seed-0 corrected
  GC-LSTM pilot, selected epoch 7, with 256 held-out per-target F1 values and
  frozen phase-window contrasts. One model and graph; no cumulative decline
  across the four repeated cycles or causal retention claim is established.
- `figures/figure10-state-diagnostics.pdf`: compact verified time-cloud
  covariance summaries for hidden and complete H+C state. Their within-motif
  PR changes have opposite directions; pooled clouds also mix motif means.
  These are not Jacobian/Floquet measurements or independent raw-state
  reanalyses. Both copied PDFs are hash-verified against manifests in
  `doc/auto/figures/figure10-gclstm-pilot-2026-10-19/`.
- `figures/probe_scale_artifact.pdf`: a noiseless invertible scalar code whose
  fixed-floor ridge probe deteriorates. It illustrates a possible confound;
  it does not re-reduce or explain away the actual pilot clouds.
- `../doc/auto/figures/prefix-acquisition-v3-endpoints.pdf`: every required
  coordinate at eight fixed 5,000-update endpoints, from the independently
  verified scalar summary. All eight full-coordinate gates fail; five
  coordinate scores pass. No independent training-seed interval is estimated.
- `../doc/auto/figures/address-acquisition-v4-endpoints.pdf`: all twelve fixed
  singleton-query endpoints with paired pinned-prefix baseline reevaluations
  at addresses 0 and 7. All eight address-7/address-15 gates pass; four
  address-0 gates fail. Different singleton weights do not establish joint
  memory, and address, lag and required load change together.

## Scientific readiness

The manuscript has complete prose, proofs, captions and figures rather than
anticipated-result placeholders. Editorial completeness does not establish
conference acceptance or resolve the outstanding research question. The
current ingredients substantially overlap established work, and all four
pilots fail native competence. The
[readiness review](../doc/auto/manuscript-review-2026-10-12.md) distinguishes
what is supported from what still requires an original result, competent
replicated models and causal controls. Independent periodicity reduction and
the October 13 acquisition audits are now complete; they preserve the failed
competence prerequisite rather than establish repetition-induced forgetting. The graph appendix adds
a verified behavioral within-motif decline, conditional state-geometry
summaries and a nonzero period-64 closure residual for one capped model.
It reproduces neither the full multi-model/five-seed T-GRAB figure nor a
historical checkpoint, and it establishes no harmful causal memory loss.
Offline W&B histories/result metadata are verified; dashboard delivery remains
unverified.
