[the plan](../../configs/research-plan.json) and the latest dated notebook entry
under `doc/`. Read [the workflow contract](references/workflow.md) when creating
an experiment, changing dependencies, verifying results, or recovering a run.

The initial assets are graph temporal manuscripts and rendered figures in
`floquet_analysis/` and `ACM___TGRAB_KDD_2026/`. These support a source audit;
they do not establish executable code, available checkpoints, or replicated
dimension-collapse measurements. Preserve preliminary findings as attributed
claims until an independent reproduction verifies them.

Choose a bounded ready node, freeze its question and acceptance criteria, carry
out authorized local work, verify artifacts, and append an evidence-bearing
handoff. Negative findings advance the DAG when their measurements are valid.
Scientific acceptance is a separate decision from exit status and schema checks.

asset recovery → graph reproduction → cross-modal pilots. Calibration and asset
recovery may run independently once the audit has fixed their contracts. Do not
reinterpret ordinary trajectory PCA rank as a Floquet result, or contractive
period multipliers as proof of representation dimension collapse. Match the
complete recurrent state, input forcing, period, and reference trajectory.
